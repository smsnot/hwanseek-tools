# Turn a monthly shift row into a calendar you can check

HWANSEEK · Five English cards · 1080 × 1350 pixels

Illustrations for reviewing a monthly shift row before calendar import. Local product checks and a downloaded calendar file were verified; no calendar-app import or phone test was executed. These are vector illustrations, not screenshots.

[Read the guide](../../guides/shift-calendar-review/README.md) for evidence, sources, and limits.

## Cards and alternate text

### Card 1: One monthly row. Dates you can check.

[PNG](card-01.png) · [SVG](card-01.svg)

Alt text: Card 1 of 5. One monthly row, dates you can check. Choose the month and work time zone, paste your own daily cells including days off, and review dates before downloading. Use an employer calendar export if available.

### Card 2: Blank cells still have dates.

[PNG](card-02.png) · [SVG](card-02.svg)

Alt text: Card 2 of 5. Blank cells still have dates. A synthetic row fragment shows day 9 as N, day 10 unresolved, and day 11 as OFF. Check the source and resolve day 10 in place rather than deleting the blank and moving later shifts.

### Card 3: Your codes. Your actual hours.

[PNG](card-03.png) · [SVG](card-03.svg)

Alt text: Card 3 of 5. Your codes, your actual hours. Confirm every code as hours or a day off; uppercase N and lowercase n differ. Starting examples are D 07:00 to 19:00, N 19:00 to 07:00 the next day, and OFF with no work event. Edit these examples to match your roster.

### Card 4: Check the midnight crossing.

[PNG](card-04.png) · [SVG](card-04.svg)

Alt text: Card 4 of 5. Check the midnight crossing. A synthetic shift starts September 30 at 22:00 and ends October 1 at 06:00. Match both dates to the source and use the work time zone. Confirm missing or repeated daylight-saving clock times instead of accepting a guess.

### Card 5: Import into a separate calendar.

[PNG](card-05.png) · [SVG](card-05.svg)

Alt text: Card 5 of 5. Import into a separate calendar. Download after reviewing the row, use a computer for Google Calendar import, and check the first, last, overnight, and OFF days. A calendar file is a snapshot; do not assume reimporting a revised roster replaces old shifts.
