# Samsung Gallery and OneDrive cards

Five HWANSEEK educational cards about downloading selected originals and continuing camera backup when Samsung Gallery direct sync ends on September 30, 2026.

The diagrams explain official documentation; they are not app screenshots or a hands-on Galaxy test. PNG cards are 1080 x 1350 (4:5), numbered 01 to 05. Matching SVG files are editable sources.

See [the guide](../../guides/samsung-gallery-onedrive/README.md) for steps, sources, and limits. Original HWANSEEK text and diagrams are provided under this repository's MIT license. Third-party product names belong to their owners.
